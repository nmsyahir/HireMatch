Safe communication
Chat Guidelines
Professional Communication

Keep conversations professional and related to the job application.

Avoid repeated messages, advertisements, or spam.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and inappropriate language are not allowed.

Privacy and Safety

Never share passwords, OTP codes, banking details, or other sensitive information.

Be cautious of payment requests or requests to communicate outside HireMatch.

Suspicious Activity

Do not open suspicious links or files, and do not send misleading job information.

Contact HireMatch support if you receive suspicious or inappropriate messages.
