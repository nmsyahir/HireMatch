How HireMatch Recommendations Work

Understand how jobs are matched and ranked for you.

HireMatch extracts text from your latest uploaded resume and compares it with available job information using TF-IDF and cosine similarity. Jobs with stronger textual similarity are ranked higher.

The percentage is a textual similarity score used to compare opportunities. Scores from 40% are Excellent, 25-39% Good, 10-24% Average, and below 10% Low. The top-ranked job is labelled Best Match only when it reaches at least 25%. This score does not represent your probability of being hired.

Matched skills are requirements found in both your resume and the job posting. Missing skills are requirements mentioned by the employer that were not detected in your latest resume.
